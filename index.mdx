Watl runs the whole restaurant from one place: the online ordering site your
customers see, the counter, the kitchen, and the drivers who take the food out.

Everyone signs in through the same door and lands on the screens their job
needs. Nothing else is shown to them.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Drivers" icon="truck" href="/roles/drivers">
    See the deliveries assigned to you and the customer's phone number.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Managers" icon="clipboard-check" href="/roles/managers">
    Run the order list during service and sign off staff checklists.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Owners" icon="store" href="/roles/owners">
    The menu, the roster, the staff, the settings and every order.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Where to start

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get your invitation">
    The owner invites you by email. The link lets you set your own password.
    See [Signing in](/signing-in).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Learn the seven order statuses">
    Every screen in Watl is built around them, and they are the words your team
    will use out loud during service. See [Order statuses](/order-statuses).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read your own page">
    Pick your role above. Each page shows the real screens with real orders on
    them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Every screenshot in this handbook is the running app with real data — a real
  restaurant, real staff accounts and six real orders. Nothing is a mockup.
</Note>
