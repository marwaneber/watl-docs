---
title: "Signing in"
description: "How to get into Watl, and where you land afterwards."
---

Everyone uses the same sign-in page. Where you go next depends on your role —
Watl sends you straight to the first screen your job needs.

## Joining by invitation

Staff accounts are not self-service. The owner invites you, and you finish the
setup yourself.

<Steps>
  <Step title="The owner sends an invitation">
    They pick your role — manager, cashier or driver — when they invite you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You open the link">
    It takes you to a page where you choose your own name and password. Drivers
    are also asked for a vehicle and a licence number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You're in">
    Your account becomes active the moment you finish. Until then the owner sees
    you listed as **Inactive**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Invitation links expire after seven days. If yours has gone stale, ask the
  owner to send another — the old link cannot be revived.
</Warning>

## The sign-in page

<Info>
  **Sign Up** in the top corner creates a whole new restaurant. It is not how
  staff join. If you work at a restaurant that already uses Watl, you need an
  invitation instead.
</Info>

- Enter your email and password. The eye icon shows the password if you need to check it.
- **Forgot your password?** sits just above the password box.

## Where you land

| Your role | You land on |
| --- | --- |
| Driver | Deliveries |
| Manager | Orders |
| Cashier | Point of sale |
| Owner / admin | Menu |

You cannot open a screen outside your role. If you type the address of one,
Watl quietly returns you to your own first screen.

## Signing out

Use the menu next to your name at the bottom of the sidebar.

<Warning>
  On a shared restaurant computer, sign out when you finish your shift. The next
  person to use it will otherwise be working under your name.
</Warning>
