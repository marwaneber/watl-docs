A manager's console is deliberately narrow: two screens, and nothing to get lost
in. **Orders** is where the shift happens. **Validator** is where you sign off
the checklists your staff worked through.

## Orders

The live order list, and the screen you will have open all shift.

  <img src="/images/manager-orders-desktop.png" alt="Manager Orders screen showing a sidebar with Orders and Validator, filter tabs, and a table of six orders with reference, customer, items, total, status badge, driver and time." />

### Reading a row

| Column | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| Order | The short reference. Read it out to a driver or a customer. |
| Customer | Their name, with the phone number underneath. |
| Items | How many things are in the order. |
| Total | What the order is worth. |
| Status | Where it has got to. See [Order statuses](/order-statuses). |
| Driver | Who is taking it, or **Unassigned**. |
| Date | When it came in. |

### What you can do

<Steps>
  <Step title="Filter to what matters">
    The tabs across the top — All, Pending, Accepted, In progress, Completed,
    Cancelled — narrow the list. During a rush, **Pending** is the one to watch.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move an order along">
    The **⋯** button at the end of a row is where you change the status and
    assign a driver.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take a phone order">
    **New Order** creates one by hand for someone who rang the restaurant.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the whole order">
    Click any row to see the items, the notes and the full history.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Assigning a driver and setting the status are two separate actions. Assigning
  somebody does **not** move the order to *Out for delivery* — do that when the
  food actually leaves.
</Note>

## Validator

Where you sign off the checklists your staff were supposed to work through —
opening checks, closing routines, cleaning rounds.

<Frame caption="One assignment waiting to be validated.">
  <img src="/images/manager-validator-desktop.png" alt="Task Validator screen showing an assignment card with a MORNING badge, a 0 of 21 checked counter, the assignee name, the checklist title, a date range and an Open assignment button." />
</Frame>

- **Each card is one assignment** — a checklist given to a person, for a shift, over a date range.
- **The counter** ("0/21 checked") is the progress through it.
- **Open assignment** walks you through the items one at a time in a full-screen stepper.
- **"No assignments scheduled"** is normal at the start of a day. Assignments come from the owner's Tasks screen.

<Info>
  The wording of a checklist is whatever the owner typed. Watl does not
  translate it — if the templates were written in Arabic or French, that is what
  you will see.
</Info>
