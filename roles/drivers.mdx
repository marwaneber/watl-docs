Your screen has one job: show you the orders that are yours, and give you the
customer's phone number. It is built for a phone.

## The three tabs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Collect — the restaurant is preparing these" icon="clock">
    Orders that have been accepted, are being cooked, or are sitting ready on
    the pass waiting for you. This is your queue.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Going — you have these with you" icon="truck">
    Orders the restaurant has handed over. They stay here until somebody inside
    closes them out.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Done — finished today" icon="circle-check">
    Completed and cancelled orders. Useful for checking back over your day.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## What's on an order card

- **The customer's name** and, underneath, the order reference.
- **Their phone number** — tap it to call. This is the important one.
- **How many items** and the **total to collect** if it is cash on delivery.
- **A note** from the customer, if they left one, in a box at the bottom.

<Warning>
  **There is no delivery address on the order.** Watl's checkout only captures a
  name and a phone number, so the address is not carried through. Phone the
  customer when you set off — that is the intended flow today, not a fault with
  your phone.
</Warning>

## Things worth knowing

<Note>
  **The screen refreshes itself every 15 seconds.** There are no push
  notifications yet, so a new order shows up within about a quarter of a minute.
  Leave the screen open during service rather than checking back into it.
</Note>

- **You only ever see your own orders.** The filtering happens on Watl's side,
  not on your phone — you cannot pull up somebody else's delivery even by
  accident.
- **You cannot mark an order delivered.** There is no button for it because
  Watl does not accept the change from a driver. Tell the counter when you drop
  it and they will close it out.
- **Nothing is missing if a tab is empty.** "Nothing to collect" means exactly
  that.

## If the screen won't load

It will tell you it could not reach the server and keep retrying every 15
seconds. Check your signal. If it still fails once you have bars, tell the
manager — it is not something you can fix from the app.
