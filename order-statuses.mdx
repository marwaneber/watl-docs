---
title: "Order statuses"
description: "The seven states every order moves through, and who changes them."
---

Every role sees the same seven statuses. They are the backbone of Watl, so they
are worth learning once — your team will end up saying them out loud during
service.

| Status | What it means | Who sets it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `PENDING` | The customer placed the order. Nobody has looked at it yet. | Set automatically at checkout |
| `ACCEPTED` | The restaurant has taken it on. | Manager, cashier or owner |
| `IN_PROGRESS` | The kitchen is cooking it. | Manager, cashier or owner |
| `READY_FOR_PICKUP` | Finished and waiting on the pass. | Manager, cashier or owner |
| `OUT_FOR_DELIVERY` | A driver has it and is on the way. | Manager, cashier or owner |
| `COMPLETED` | Delivered or collected. Done. | Manager, cashier or owner |
| `CANCELLED` | Called off. | Manager, cashier or owner |

## Who can change a status

<Warning>
  **Drivers cannot change any status.** Watl only accepts status changes from a
  manager, cashier or owner. A driver can see exactly where an order has got to,
  but marking it delivered is somebody else's job.
</Warning>

This is deliberate, and it is worth telling a restaurant up front when you set
them up: somebody on the inside has to close each order out. In practice the
person on the counter does it when the driver confirms the drop.
